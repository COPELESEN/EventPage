Universal Design for Learning in Universities:
Advancing to Personalized Learning Excellence for All Students

Total: Around 5 - 8 hours (asynchronous online), flexible learning pacing.

🎯 Target Participants

  • UGC-funded university academic staff
  • Teaching-track faculty
  • Lecturers / Assistant Professors
  • Programme leaders
  • Instructional Designers
  • SEN Support Team

💡 Intended Learning Outcomes

  • Explain why implementing UDL is important for personalizing learning experiences for university students.
  • Apply the three principles and nine guidelines of UDL 3.0 in higher education contexts.
  • Utilize Generative AI and accessibility technologies to enhance personalized learning.
